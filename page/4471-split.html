The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Any date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April goes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for.
  • hint: tenants on booking-v3 print MM/DD/YYYY under the form’s date field. Their people type the month first, as told, and the form reads the field day first. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant. The parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day; day and month are not swapped. It depends on where the reader is: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A date-only value is going through a timezone conversion. The desk listed these reports in its notes, then filed them under the tenant’s cause.

Why it looked like one

  • Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser; the cause depends on the tenant. Every report from an en-US browser was tagged hint, wherever its tenant is.
  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line. Every subject here is some form of “wrong date”, so three bugs went into one ticket.
  • The cause table on #4471 counts the one-day moves under parser and hint, and shows hint as merged into the wrong ticket, while the page above it says one issue, one fix.

What to do

  • Track each problem on its own ticket (links in the table), not on #4471.
  • Place reports by tenant and what the body shows, not by intake tag or subject.
